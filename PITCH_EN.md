# Gamma AI Prompt — CookEasy Pitch (English, simple)

Paste everything below into Gamma (Generate from text/outline) to create a short investor
pitch deck. The language is kept simple on purpose, so it's easy to read out loud during a live
presentation.

---

Create a short presentation (8-10 slides max), professional and direct, to pitch "CookEasy" to
an investor. No filler — every slide should say something an investor cares about. Use simple,
clear English (short sentences). Use this content:

**Slide 1 — Cover**
CookEasy — Cook with what you already have.
A web app that turns your pantry into real recipes, with zero food waste.

**Slide 2 — The problem**
- Families throw away 30-40% of the food they buy.
- People don't know what to cook with what they have, so they order delivery or buy more food.
- Recipe apps don't know what's in your kitchen. They just show a generic catalog.

**Slide 3 — The solution**
CookEasy connects what you have at home with what you can cook today:
- Scan or type your ingredients → get recipes instantly, ranked by best match.
- Keep a real pantry inventory, with expiration dates.
- The app warns you before food goes bad, and suggests recipes to use it in time.

**Slide 4 — What makes us different**
- **Barcode scanning** (phone camera, free, no paid API): add real products in seconds, no
  typing.
- **Smart pantry with expiration alerts**: nobody else solves this well.
- **"What am I missing"**: every suggested recipe shows exactly which ingredients you don't
  have, with a button to add them to your shopping list.
- **Built-in shopping list**: closes the loop, from pantry to kitchen.

**Slide 5 — How it works (user flow)**
1. Scan or add what's in your pantry.
2. CookEasy shows you possible recipes, starting with what expires soonest.
3. Missing something? Add it to your shopping list in one tap.
4. Cook, rate the recipe, repeat.

**Slide 6 — Who it's for**
Students, busy professionals, families, and beginner cooks — anyone who wants to stop asking
"what do I cook today?" and stop throwing food away.

**Slide 7 — A real product, not just an idea**
- Ingredient-based recipe search with smart matching.
- Full account system (users and admins), with favorites, user-submitted recipes, star ratings
  and reviews.
- Complete admin dashboard: manage users, recipes, categories, and the food database.
- Real photo upload for every recipe.
- Built on a modern, scalable stack (Next.js, a real relational database), ready to grow to
  thousands of users without a rebuild.

**Slide 8 — Business model (potential)**
- Freemium: free basics, paid subscription for advanced features (weekly meal planner,
  unlimited scanning, premium recommendations).
- Supermarket partnerships: shopping suggestions with direct product links.
- Anonymous, aggregated food-consumption data for food brands.

**Slide 9 — Why now**
Food waste and saving money at home are bigger concerns than ever. CookEasy isn't "another
recipe app" — it's a real savings tool with a daily-use hook (the pantry updates constantly).

**Slide 10 — Closing**
CookEasy: from pantry to plate, with zero waste.
